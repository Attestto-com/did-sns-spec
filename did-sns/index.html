Alias-anchored identity. The did:sns method binds W3C Decentralized Identifiers to human-readable SNS domains (alice.crbank.sol). Web3 native, Web2 transparent — end users interact with readable aliases while the specification guarantees interoperability, privacy, and compliance across institutions, jurisdictions, and blockchains.
